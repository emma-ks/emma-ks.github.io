## Journal
*Nature* 584, no. 7820 (2020): 262-267

## Abstract
This study estimates the effect of large-scale anti-contagion policies on the COVID-19 pandemic across six countries. Using reduced-form econometric methods, we find that anti-contagion policies have significantly slowed the growth rate of infections, preventing hundreds of millions of additional cases.
