## Journal
*Scientific Data* 9, no. 1 (2022): 1-17

## Abstract
This paper presents a harmonized dataset of nitrogen and phosphorous concentrations across the Mississippi/Atchafalaya River Basin spanning nearly four decades. The dataset enables researchers to study long-term trends in water quality and identify potential sources of nonpoint source pollution in one of the largest river systems in North America.
